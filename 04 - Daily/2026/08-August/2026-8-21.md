After packing it, I went with her to drop it off at a collection point, where it would be forwarded to our relatives. She rode an electric bike and led the way while I followed her from behind on an old motorbike.

I rode the old bike to the drop-off point, but on my way home, I ran out of petrol in the middle of the road. Since I didn't have any cash and my phone with me, I had no choice but to push it on my own.

She paid for some petrol for me, so I could get home much faster without having to push the bike the rest of the way. I'm deeply grateful to her, but at the same time, I really regret not asking for her name and her contact details. I wish I could thank her properly and return the favor someday. I even thought that if she were ever pulled over by traffic police, she could contact me, and perhaps my father, who knows some local officers and might be able to help her. Now I feel really awful because I owe someone a favor and have no way of repaying her.

I've promised myself that I will never let my emotions get the better of me ever again, and I won't talk back to someone like my dad that way again.
