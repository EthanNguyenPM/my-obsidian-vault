A person who comes to mind is a friend of mine from the Philippines, whom I met on Discord. We used to study together online just for fun, so studying was also a way for us to spend time together.

At one point, I needed to take a break from Discord. When I contacted her afterwards, she didn’t reply. I wasn’t sure what had happened, so I waited, hoping I’d hear from her eventually.

But as the days turned into weeks, I started to feel disappointed and a bit sad. In the end, I went about three months without hearing from her. I didn’t know whether she was busy with other things or had simply stopped using the platform. Without a reply, there wasn’t really any way for me to know.

I think the uncertainty was what made the situation difficult. When you only know someone online, it can be hard to tell whether they’re just taking a break or whether you’ll actually speak to them again. I kept hoping she’d come back, but I didn’t know when that would happen.

Eventually, she did get back to me, and I was really happy to see her message. Even though the wait had been upsetting, I was glad we could talk again. I still don’t know exactly why she took so long to reply, but hearing from her after all that time meant a lot to me.
