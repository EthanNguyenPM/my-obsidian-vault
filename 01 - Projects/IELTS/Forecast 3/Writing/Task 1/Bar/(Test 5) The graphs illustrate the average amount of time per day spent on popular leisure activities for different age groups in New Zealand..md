_The bar charts compare the average time spent on reading for pleasure and listening to music across 5 different age groups in New Zealand.

_Overall, reading time is progressively higher among the older age groups, whereas listening time is lowest among those aged 35 to 54. People spend more time listening to music than reading for pleasure in all age groups except the 35-44-year-olds, who devote equal amounts of time to both activities.

_Regarding reading, people aged 55 and above record the highest figure, at approximately 70 minutes daily. Those aged 45 to 54 and 35 to 44 spend 50 and 45 minutes respectively on this activity. The figure for 25-34-year-olds is lower, at 35 minutes, while the youngest group spends just 20 minutes reading each day.

_Listening to music shows a different pattern. Those aged 15 to 24 spend the most time on this activity, at 80 minutes per day, followed by the oldest group at 75 minutes. People aged 25 to 34 and 45 to 54 each listen for an hour daily, whereas the 35-44 group records the lowest figure, at 45 minutes. The largest gap between the two activities occurs among the youngest people, whose listening time is four times their reading time.
