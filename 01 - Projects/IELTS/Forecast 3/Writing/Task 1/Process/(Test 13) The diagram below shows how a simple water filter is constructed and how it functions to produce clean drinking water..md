_The diagram illustrates the construction of a simple water filter and explains how it produces potable water. 

_Overall, the system comprises a filtering barrel connected to a storage drum. Water passes down through three layers of filtering material before being transferred into the second container, from which it can be drawn through a tap. 

_The filter is housed in the plastic barrel with a removable plastic lid. Inside the barrel, sand forms the uppermost filtering layer, charcoal occupies the middle layer, and gravel lies at the bottom. The barrel is located at a higher level than the storage drum. A pipe connects the lower section of the barrel to the storage drum, which also has a lid and a tap near its base.

_To operate the system, the plastic lid is removed, and water is poured into the top. The water then permeates down through the sand, charcoal, and gravel, undergoing filtration as it passes through these layers. Once it reaches the bottom, the filtered water travels through the connecting pipe into the storage drum. Finally, the stored water is dispensed through the tap for drinking.
