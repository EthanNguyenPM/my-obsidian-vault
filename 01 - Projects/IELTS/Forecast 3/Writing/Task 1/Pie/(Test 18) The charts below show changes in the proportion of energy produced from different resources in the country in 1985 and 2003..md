The pie charts compare the percentages of various energies generated in 1985 and 2003 in the country.

Overall, the country recorded the highest proportion of oil energy throughout the period. While the figures for coal, natural gas, and other renewable energy sources increased, those for the remaining sources declined over an 18-year period, except for hydropower.

In 1985, Oil represented the largest share among other energy resources at 52%, followed by nuclear energy and natural gas at 22% and 13%, respectively. While the proportion for hydropower made up 4%, the natural gas figure constituted twice as much as that of hydropower. Other renewable energy was the lowest portion, just only 1%.

In 2003, although the percentage of oil energy had plummeted over the years, its figure continued to remain the greatest share. Natural gas and coal energy experienced a climb in the following 18 years and ranked second and third with 23% and 22%. By contrast, nuclear power showed the largest decline from 22% to just 8%. Both Hydropower and other renewable energy accounted for 4%, with the former's figure remaining unchanged over the period.
