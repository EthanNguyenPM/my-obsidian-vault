_The plans compare the layout of a student common room five years ago with its current arrangement.

_Overall, the room has been adapted to accommodate a wider range of activities, with new facilities for laptop use and watching television. The seating arrangement has also changed, while most of the kitchen facilities remain along the upper wall.

_Five years ago, the room contained four circular tables, each surrounded by six chairs. Along the upper wall, there was a sink, a cupboard, two benches, a microwave and a refrigerator. The entrance was situated on the left-hand side.

_Today, the sink, cupboard, refrigerator and one of the original benches remain in place. The other bench has been replaced by a second microwave, and a drinks machine has been added below the refrigerator.

_The four circular tables have been replaced by two circular tables seating six people each and a rectangular table with ten chairs. A bench for laptops now occupies the lower central part of the room. Meanwhile, the right-hand side has been furnished with three armchairs and a television, creating a separate seating area. The entrance remains in its original position.
