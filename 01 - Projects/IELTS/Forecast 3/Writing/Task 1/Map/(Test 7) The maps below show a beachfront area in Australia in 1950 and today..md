_The maps compare the layout of a beachfront area in Australia in 1950 with its present-day appearance.

_Overall, the area has been developed with additional parking, dining, and recreational facilities, alongside improved road access. While the playground has disappeared and one swimming pool has been enlarged, several original features have remained unchanged.

_In 1950, the road from the city ran along the northern edge of the area and ended at a car park to the east of the pavilion. Today, this car park remains, but the road has been extended eastwards and then southwards to reach the lighthouse. A second car park has replaced the playground in the northwestern part of the site. The dining tables and pavilion remain in their original locations between the two car parks

_Near the beach, the western swimming pool has been enlarged from 25 to 50 meters. In addition, a restaurant has been constructed to its west, with a new surf club nearby. Another surf club has been added beside the eastern pool, which is 25 meters long. The lighthouse at the eastern end of the beach has also been retained.
