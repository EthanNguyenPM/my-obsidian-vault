_The diagrams compare the South Wing of Walton Museum in 2008 and 2012, following its redevelopment.

_Overall, the wing was expanded vertically and provided with several new visitor facilities. Existing rooms were reorganized, the reception and the cloakroom were combined, and a lift replaced the stairs.

_In 2008, visitors approached the entrance along a path on the left. The special exhibition room occupied the lower-left section, beside the shop. The reception was directly above the shop, while the reading room occupied the upper-right section. Below it was a separate cloakroom, with stairs situated between this room and the shop.

_By 2012, an entrance hall had been added above the special exhibition room, with a terrace directly overhead. The shop remained on the lowest level in the central section, while the space above it accommodated a combined reception and cloakroom. A new restaurant was located above these facilities.

_On the right-hand side, the former cloakroom was replaced by a children’s play area, while the reading room remained on the level above. Two additional rooms were constructed higher up: a self-service room and, above it, an exhibition room. A lift connected the different levels in place of the original stairs.
