Today was quite a rollercoaster. I woke up at 8:00 a.m. since my friend and I had agreed to meet up at the coffee shop where we used to hang out with each other. So that's why I got up much earlier than usual. However, my friend didn't keep her word, I kept texting to ask her whether she was ready to head out, but all I got was silence. About two hours later, she messaged me saying that she was so sorry and would be there in no time. Although I was fairly frustrated, I decided to give her the benefit of the doubt.

Even so, despite all unexpected interruptions, I had a great, gossipy chat with the babysitter, who is five years younger than I am, yet mature and hardworking, which are what I really admire about her. At the end of the day, she even apologized for all the inconvenient things that came up for me throughout the day.
