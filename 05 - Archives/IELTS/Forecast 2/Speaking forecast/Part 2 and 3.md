### 2.1 What short-term jobs do young people do in other countries?

Young people often take short-term jobs in areas such as hospitality, retail, tourism, and agriculture. For example, they may work as waiters, hotel staff, shop assistants, tour guides, fruit pickers, or festival workers. Some also take temporary office jobs, internships, or summer-camp positions. These jobs are popular because they allow young people to earn money, gain work experience, and improve their language skills without making a long-term commitment.

### 2.2 What kind of work can young people do in foreign countries?

Young people can do a wide range of jobs in foreign countries, depending on their qualifications, language skills, and visa conditions. For example, students often work part-time in cafés, restaurants, supermarkets, or hotels. Those with professional skills may find jobs in fields such as information technology, engineering, healthcare, or education. Some young people also work as farm workers, delivery drivers, caregivers, or English teachers.
