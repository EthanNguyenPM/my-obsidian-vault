
| Chức năng        | Cấu trúc                               | Ví dụ                                                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lý do (Why)      | **Given + N / the fact that…**         | **Given the high cost of equipment**, many schools cannot offer sports. |
| Lý do (Why)      | **Due to / Owing to / Because of + N** | **Due to limited funding**, facilities remain poor.                     |
| Lý do (Why)      | **V-ing / Having + V3**                | **Having invested heavily in training**, the country won many medals.   |
| Thời gian (When) | **In + thời điểm**                     | **In the past decade**, investment in sport has risen sharply.          |
| Thời gian (When) | **During / Over / Since + N**          | **Over the last few years**, more stadiums have been built.             |
| Thời gian (When) | **On + V-ing / After + V-ing**         | **After completing the training**, athletes join the national team.     |
| Nơi chốn (Where) | **In / At / Across + nơi chốn**        | **In many developing countries**, sport facilities are limited.         |
| Cách thức (How)  | **By + V-ing / With + N**              | **By investing in facilities**, governments can nurture talent.         |
| Mục đích (Why)   | **To + V / In order to + V**           | **To produce top athletes**, countries must invest in training.         |
| Điều kiện        | **With / Without + N**                 | **Without proper facilities**, young talent may be wasted.              |
| Nhượng bộ        | **Despite / In spite of + N**          | **Despite the budget cuts**, the team performed well.                   |
| Phạm vi          | **In terms of / In the case of + N**   | **In terms of public health**, sport brings clear benefits.             |
| Tương phản       | **Unlike / In contrast to + N**        | **Unlike team sports**, tennis relies on individual skill.              |
