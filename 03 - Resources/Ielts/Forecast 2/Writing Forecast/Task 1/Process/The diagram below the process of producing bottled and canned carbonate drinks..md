The process illustrates the steps of producing bottled and canned carbonate beverages.

Overall, this process consists of five stages, commencing with water treatment and ending with the distribution of final products.

The first stage begins when raw water is drawn from the water supply, which is followed by a series of treatments, including filtration, softening, and application of chemicals. From there, the purified water is pumped directly to electric heating systems and thus enters the second stage of the process. After being heated, the water passes through a cooling pipe that reduces the temperature, and then is transitioned into a machine where it is carbonated.

In the third stage, the carbonated liquid is transferred to a mixing tank before undergoing constant mixing with the addition of coloring, syrup, and flavor. To ensure the carbonated water is drinkable, it is filtered once again in the fourth stage, and afterward, it is poured into different cans or bottles and sealed. Eventually, the process concludes with the packaging and delivery of the end products to retail outlets such as purveyors and supermarkets.
