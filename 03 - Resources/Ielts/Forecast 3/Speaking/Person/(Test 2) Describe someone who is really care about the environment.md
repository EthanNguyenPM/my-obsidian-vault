For example, whenever he notices litter lying around, whether it's in an alley, a university corridor, or a classroom, he picks it up and puts it in a bin. Although it might seem like a simple thing, I appreciate the fact that he takes the initiative instead of waiting for someone to deal with it.

He's also talked to me about joining environmental clubs. One idea we've discussed is making posters and putting them up around the university to raise awareness of environmental issues. The messages could focus on simple habits, like putting rubbish in a bin rather than leaving it in a classroom. I think practical reminders can make the idea of protecting the environment feel more relevant to students' daily lives.

Another thing he encourages is using electric bikes as an environmentally friendly way to get around. That could be particularly useful for short journeys where cycling is a practical option.

What I admire most is his willingness to do something, even on a small scale. For me, he's a good example of how caring about the environment can become part of your everyday routine.
