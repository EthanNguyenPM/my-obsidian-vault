_The bar chart compares the average number of minutes per person per day that men and women in Great Britain spend on six categories of household tasks.

_Overall, women devote more time to most activities, particularly cooking and cleaning, whereas men spend longer on gardening and pet care, as well as maintenance and odd jobs. Cooking is the most time-consuming category for women, while gardening and pet care account for the greatest amount of men’s time.

_Women spend 74 minutes daily on cooking, baking and washing up, compared with 30 minutes for men. Cleaning and house tidying show the largest gender gap, with women spending 58 minutes on these tasks and men just 13. Women also devote more time to caring for and playing with their children, at 45 minutes versus 20. The corresponding figures for washing, ironing and sewing clothes are 25 minutes for women and only two for men.

_By contrast, men spend 49.5 minutes per day on gardening and pet care, more than twice the female figure of 21 minutes. Maintenance and odd jobs also take up more time for men, at 26 minutes daily, compared with 9.5 minutes for women.
