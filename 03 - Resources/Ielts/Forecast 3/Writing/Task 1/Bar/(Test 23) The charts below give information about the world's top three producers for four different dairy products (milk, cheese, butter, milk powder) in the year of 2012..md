The bar chart showed the various dairy productions in the world's top three producers in the year of 2012, measured in metric tons.

Overall, milk was produced in far greater quantities among all four daily products shown. The EU featured among the top three daily producers in every category, and led milk and cheese production. India was the largest producer of butter, while China ranked first in milk powder production. 

Regarding milk, the EU produced 142,970 metric tons, followed by India at 127,000. The United States ranked third, with an output of 90,038 metric tons. The EU also led cheese production, at 7,120 metric tons, compared with 4,926 in the US. Brazil’s figure was considerably lower, at just 700 metric tons.

India dominated butter production, producing 4,500 metric tons, more than twice the EU’s output of 2,040. The US followed with 810 metric tons. By contrast, milk powder production showed a much smaller gap between the two leading producers: China recorded 1,200 metric tons, only 90 more than New Zealand. The EU occupied third place, with 770 metric tons.
