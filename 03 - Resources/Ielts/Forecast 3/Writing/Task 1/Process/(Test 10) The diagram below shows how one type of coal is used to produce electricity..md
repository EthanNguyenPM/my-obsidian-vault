_The diagram illustrates how electricity is generated from coal through a process involving gasification.

_Overall, coal is first extracted and crushed before being converted into gas. This gas is then cooled and cleaned before entering a power plant, where electricity is produced for distribution. Waste heat is released during both cooling and electricity generation.

_Initially, coal is extracted from a mine and fed into a crusher, which breaks it into smaller pieces. The crushed material is then transported by truck to a gasifier. Heat is supplied to this unit to convert the coal into gas.

_Next, the gas passes to a cooling facility, where its temperature is reduced and waste heat is discharged. It subsequently undergoes cleaning before being transferred to a gas power plant. At this stage, the processed gas is used to generate electricity, with further waste heat produced as a by-product. Finally, the electricity is supplied to buildings and distributed through a power network.
