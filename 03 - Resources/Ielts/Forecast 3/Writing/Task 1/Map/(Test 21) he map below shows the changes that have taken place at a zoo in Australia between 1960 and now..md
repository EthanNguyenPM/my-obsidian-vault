The maps illustrate how a zoo in Australia has changed between 1960 and the present.

Overall, the zoo has been reorganized, with changes to several attractions and the addition of new access and transport facilities. Meanwhile, the main paths, picnic area and Australian animals enclosure have largely retained their original positions. 

In 1960, the harbor was located in the northern part of the zoo. A main road ran diagonally from the southwest to the middle of the waterfront, with three branches leading off. One branch led westward to the Australian animals enclosure, with the African animals enclosure to its north. Of the two eastward branches, the lower one led to the chimpanzees' enclosure, while the upper one passed the birds' enclosure before reaching the seal show. The gift shop stood in the lower central section, and the picnic area occupied the eastern side.

Today, the wild animals occupy the former African animals' enclosure. The African animals' and seal show has been relocated to the central area, flanked by two east-facing side roads, while the separate birds' and chimpanzees' enclosures are no longer shown. The upper path now leads to the cable car station, connected to another station near the renovated gift shop. Two entrances have also been added: one at the end of the main road in the southwest and another beside the harbor where a ferry is shown. Meanwhile, the Australian animals enclosure, picnic area and gift shop remain in approximately the same locations, although the gift shop's appearance has changed slightly.
