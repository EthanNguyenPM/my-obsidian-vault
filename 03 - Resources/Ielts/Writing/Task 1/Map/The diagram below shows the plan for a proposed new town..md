The diagram illustrates the proposed layout for the new settlement.

Overall, the town will be designed in a concentric layout with good accessibility. Additionally, some municipal amenities are concentrated in the centre, while residential areas occupy the largest portion, and industrial zones are placed towards the outskirts.

The heart of the town lies in a central square, featuring two bus stations and three parking areas. This core is encircled by a network of roads that extend radially in several main directions and connect to a ring road, thereby dividing the surrounding inner town into distinct sectors containing housing and recreational facilities.

The outer regions of the town comprise various sections, facilitating additional residential and industrial areas, while the latter are situated in the north-western and south-eastern quadrants of the layout, minimizing their impact on residential living. Furthermore, the South and the North also plan to construct leisure establishments, particularly distributing the most in the North. Finally, the road systems are constructed to segment the outskirts into distinct clusters, connecting to the ring road in the center.
