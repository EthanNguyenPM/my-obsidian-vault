Today, the current iteration of the beachfront has experienced substantial transformation. First and foremost, the road has been extended from the car park junction eastward, then bends down to the lighthouse. The former playground was now removed to make way for an enlarged car park, while a set of dining tables and a pavilion remain in the same position. On the southern side, surf club facilities are erected on both sides of the beach. Notably, the western side has shown big changes, with the emergence of a restaurant and the expansion of the current swimming pool, increasing from 25 meters to 50 meters.
