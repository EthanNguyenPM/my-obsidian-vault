Below the intersection, a row of shops stretched westward along the northern side of the coastal road, while a hotel stood to the east of the intersection. On the contrary, the present village has been transformed considerably. The farmland and forest park have been completely replaced by a golf course in the north and tennis courts to its south. In addition, former shops have been converted into a row of restaurants, and a car park has been added to the hotel's east side. The fishing port and fish market have been demolished entirely, giving way to a row of seafront apartments.
