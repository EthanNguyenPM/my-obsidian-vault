The table delineates the proportion of parliament and management positions held by women across 5 various countries, namely Canada, Norway, Tobago and Trinidad, Uruguay, and Italy, in the course of 2000.

Overall, women were strongly represented in management than in parliament in every country, with the figures outnumbering the seats held by men in Norway and Italy. In addition, Norway had the highest proportion of female parliamentarians, while Italy recorded the largest share of women in management, albeit one of the lowest figures for parliament. 

In terms of parliament, Norway ranked first, at 36 percent. Canada followed behind at 28 percent, while Tobago and Trinidad accounted for 19 percent of women. Italy and Uruguay had much smaller proportions, at 10 and 8 percent, respectively.

The pattern for management was quite different. Italy had the highest figure, at 54 percent, just ahead of Norway at 52 percent. Tobago and Trinidad recorded a relatively strong result, with women making up 40 percent of managers. Canada and Uruguay had the lowest shares in this category, both at 37%. The largest gap between the two areas was seen in Italy, where the percentage of female managers was 44 points higher than that of parliament leaders.
