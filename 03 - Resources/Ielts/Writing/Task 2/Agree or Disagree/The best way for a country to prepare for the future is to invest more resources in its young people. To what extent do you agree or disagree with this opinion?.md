The future development of the country largely depends on how well its younger generation is educated and supported. Some people believe that governments must allocate more resources to young people in order to prepare for a better future, while opponents contend that other demographics should be factored in as well. In this essay, I strongly agree with the former view, as investing in youngsters can improve economic growth and create a more stable society.

Firstly, minors are the future workforce, innovators and leaders of society. By investing in education, vocational training and professional skills, they can become more capable and productive in the future. Countries such as South Korea and Singapore, for instance, have invested significantly in education, which has helped them develop highly skilled workers and stronger economies. In addition, offering scholarships and job opportunities can encourage the youth to contribute positively to society instead of becoming unemployed and leading decadent lifestyles. 

Secondly, investing in young people also helps reduce social problems in the long term. Crimes and social issues are closely linked to poverty, unemployment, and the absence of education among young demographics. Through channeling more government expenditure into sports facilities, community programs, and mental health services, governments can guide younger generations toward healthier lifestyles and better decision-making. Consequently, society might become much safer and more stable in the years ahead.

Nevertheless, several people argue that regulatory bodies should also pay enough attention to other groups, because they require healthcare and financial support as well. Although this view is understandable, I believe that investment in young people yields greater long-term advantages, since they will become the main contributors to the economy and society.

In conclusion, I completely agree that it is young people who should be given greater investment and support for the future. Better education, stronger career opportunities, and social support for them can lead to economic prosperity and social stability.
