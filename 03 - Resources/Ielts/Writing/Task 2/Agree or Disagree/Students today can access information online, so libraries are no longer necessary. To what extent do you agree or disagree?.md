It is argued that the widespread availability of technology renders libraries obsolete. In this essay, I strongly disagree with this viewpoint, as libraries continue to serve essential functions that technology simply cannot replicate, particularly in terms of equitable access, information quality, and community learning environment.

To begin with, the internet has infiltrated every aspect of people's lives, and a majority of students in urban areas now have some form of online access. However, access to a smartphone with limited data to research is fundamentally different from the kind of stable, high-speed connectivity required for serious academic research, including downloading journal articles, streaming educational content
