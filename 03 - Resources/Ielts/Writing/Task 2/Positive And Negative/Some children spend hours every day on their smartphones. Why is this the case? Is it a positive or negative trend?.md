The contemporary landscape of young people dedicating their valuable time to smartphones has become increasingly prevalent in modern society. While this trend offers certain advantages in terms of connectivity and access to information, I firmly believe this phenomenon presents a predominantly detrimental development, primarily due to its adverse effects on physical well-being and educational attainment.

The proliferation of smartphone usage among adolescents brings health ramifications that cannot be overlooked. The addictive nature of digital entertainment, ranging from gaming platforms to endless scrolling on social media applications like TikTok, Facebook, encourages sedentary behavior patterns. Consequently, children increasingly forsake outdoor activities and physical exercise in favor of screen time, leading to alarming health complications. 

Excessive use of mobile devices by children brings substantial drawbacks for them. Health Issues and Academic performance present a major concern, as digital phones offer numerous means of entertainment, including playing games, watching endless videos, and using social media platforms like TikTok, Facebook, and gaming apps. These things capture the attention of children, who are easily drawn to them, causing them to spend valuable time on their smartphones instead of engaging in physical activities and focusing on their academic performance. Leading to many health issues, namely diabetes and cardiovascular diseases,  and poor results from pedagogical schools.
