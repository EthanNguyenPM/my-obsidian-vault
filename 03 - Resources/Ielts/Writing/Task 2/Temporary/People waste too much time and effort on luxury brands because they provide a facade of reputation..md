Luxury goods are often associated with excellent craftsmanship, uniqueness, and high social status. These qualities appeal to universal human desires to feel valued and recognized in society. Many people rely on others' perceptions of their luxury possessions to signal their personal success and social standing. For instance, an individual with a decent income opts to spend a significant portion of their earnings on a luxury watch. This decision is not merely driven by a genuine appreciation for timepieces, but rather by what the watch symbolizes in terms of achievements and prestige. This example illustrates psychological human needs for social approval, which explains why many individuals are willing to invest a significant time and money in luxury goods to maintain and project a desirable social image.
