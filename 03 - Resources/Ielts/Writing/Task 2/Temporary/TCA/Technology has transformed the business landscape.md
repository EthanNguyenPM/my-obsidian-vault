Prior to the widespread adoption of digital technology, numerous businesses were confined to their domestic markets, as expanding beyond national borders posed significant financial and logistical challenges. Marketing efforts were largely restricted to traditional forms of advertising, including billboards, posters, flyers, and word-of-mouth promotion. The advent of technology has significantly streamlined international expansion and business operations. Nowadays, companies can penetrate global markets through e-commerce platforms and digital marketing strategies, facilitating direct and immediate communication with potential foreign customers. Consequently, small enterprises are now able to compete on a global scale, demonstrating how technology has democratized access to business opportunities worldwide.
