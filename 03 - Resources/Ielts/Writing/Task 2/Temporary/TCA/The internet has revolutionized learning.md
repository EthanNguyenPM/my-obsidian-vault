This situation made it particularly challenging for disadvantaged learners, thereby stifling their academic potential and their ability to attain higher levels of proficiency. Nevertheless, with the rapid proliferation of digital technology, many conventional methods have been gradually supplemented or replaced by online platforms or web-based resources, significantly enhancing their performance and efficiency. As a result, such study materials ranging from ebooks to online courses have become increasingly widespread and readily available.
