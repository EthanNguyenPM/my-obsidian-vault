In the past, shopping often involved visiting multiple physical stores in search of desired products, and transactions were often time-consuming and inefficient. However, with the rapid proliferation of technology, e-commerce platforms have rendered this process unnecessary by streamlining the entire purchasing experience. Nowadays, customers can browse a profusion of products from various retailers, read reviews, and place orders without having to queue for long periods. This paradigm shift has significantly enhanced convenience while expanding customers' choices and intensifying market competition. Consequently, customers' behaviors and expectations have evolved substantially, as they now demand fast, more efficient, and more diverse shopping experiences.
