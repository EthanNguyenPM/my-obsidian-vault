In many developing countries, in which education infrastructures are still limited, the integration of technology into the national economy remains limited, coercing workers to rely heavily on manual labor that yields low income and faces intense market competition. A high-quality education system can address this issue by enhancing the knowledge and skills of the workforce. Education equips individuals with the qualifications needed to access jobs in modern fields such as technology or engineering, thereby increasing personal income and fostering national economic growth. However, while education is crucial, sustainable development is difficult to achieve without other factors, such as stable policies or robust welfare systems. For instance, despite significant investments in education, South Africa continues to grapple with poverty and unemployment. This underscores the importance of complementing education with appropriate regulations to maximize its effectiveness.

In many developing countries where educational infrastructure is still inadequate, the technological integration into the economy is often limited, leaving workers heavily reliant on manual labor, which typically results in low wages and intense market competition. A high-level education system can address this issue by enhancing the knowledge and skills of the workforce. Education equips individuals with the qualifications required to access employment in some sectors, such as technology and engineering, thereby increasing personal income and stimulating national economic growth. However, while education is undeniably essential, sustainable development is difficult to achieve in isolation. It also depends on stable governance, sound economic policies, and robust welfare social systems. Take South Africa, for example, despite substantial investments in education, the country still struggle with poverty and unemployment due to deep-rooted structural and socio-economic challenges. This example underscore the importance of complementing educational reform with broader institutional and policy improvements to maximize its impact.
