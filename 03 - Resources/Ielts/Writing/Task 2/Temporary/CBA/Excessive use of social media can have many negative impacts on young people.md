Excessive use of social media

In the digital age of today, social media platforms has infiltrated deeply in daily lives of young people, encompassing communication, entertainment, education and professional networking. Many individuals maintain personal account to participate in these social ecosystem. While these platforms offer global connectivity and create opportunities for information exchanges and personal or commercial brand development, their benefits represent only part of the broader issue. Excessive reliance on social media among the youth can generate substantial drawbacks. Firstly, over-dependence on virtual communication may hinder the development of face-to-face interpersonal skills, making it more challenging to establish and sustain a meaningful relationship during formative years. Secondly, a vast volume of user-generated contents is not always accurate and reliable, exposing young users to misinformation and impairing their ability to critically evaluate information. Finally, prolonged engagement in online interactions may undermine academic performance and diminish time allocated to physical activity and rest, thereby increasing the risk of mental and physical problems.

Therefore, while social media offer undeniable advantages, fostering responsible usage and enhancing digital literacy are essential to minimizing its adverse effects on younger generations.
