Social media platforms serve as powerful conduits for human interconnection, enabling a seamless exchange of information and conferring a space for self-expression. These functionalities directly cater to human psychological needs, particularly desires for social interaction and affirmation. For example, when individuals share photographs or post social updates on these media networks and subsequently receive likes and comments, they often experience a sense of appreciation and acceptance. The features of web-based social sites are inextricably linked to the psychological needs of users, which explains why these platforms attract substantial time, attention, and emotional investment. As a result, this dynamic has the propensity to create a positive feedback loop, reinforcing a facade of contentment by intensifying the fear of missing out
