Recently, I've come to realize that my diet has been severely deficient in essential nutrients, particularly vitamins and fiber. Whenever I had lunch or dinner, for instance, I always reached for meat-based dishes instead of veggies. I'm not entirely sure why I developed this habit, but perhaps meat tastes more satisfying, or I've been too lazy to think about eating mindfully.

What concerns me the most is that this kind of imbalanced eating pattern could take a toll on my health in the long run. I've already noticed a few signs. I feel more sluggish than usual in the afternoon, and my skin doesn't look as healthy as it used to. These are warning signs that I really shouldn't overlook.

Going forward, I want to make a conscious effort to include greens in every meal. Even simple changes, like adding a salad side or swapping a meat dish for a tofu or an egg-based alternative, could make a meaningful difference. I also plan to keep some fruit nearby as a snack so that I'm not tempted to reach for junk food between meals. To be honest, I think it wouldn't be easy to break this habit, but I think if I stick to it, my body will thank me eventually.
