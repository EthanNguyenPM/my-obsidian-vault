Today I learned several useful vocabulary items that can be applied in everyday conversations. Let's review them to reinforce my understanding. One term describes a person who is somewhat aggressive and prone to criticizing others. This adjective is particularly handy when portraying someone with such traits. For example, pugnacious people are often noticeable by our surroundings, as their confrontational behavior is easier to identify.

There is one synonym with a comparable nuance: Bellicose. I have encountered this word several times, but I had completely forgotten it until now.
