## What qualities make someone a role model?

I believe there are many qualities, but one that really stands out is diligence. No matter what fields they are known for, role models are usually people who work incredibly hard to reach their goals. Take Cristia Rolnado, for example. Although he is already in his 40s, he continues to train relentlessly to maintain his fitness and performance. That kind of discipline is exactly what makes people look up to him.

## Why should children learn from role models?

I'd say one reason is that role models have a lot of positive qualities that society values, like discipline and a growth mindset. So the kids can look up to them, it can really benefit their development. Another reason is that role models help children stay away from negative influences. You know, with so many temptations around these days, having someone positive to follow and guide them towards better decisions

## Who can influence children more? parents or teachers?

I think both have a strong influence on children, but if I had to choose. I'd lean towards parents a bit more. You know they live under the same roof and are involved in most of the kids' daily activities, so they shape the way a child thinks and behaves quite significantly. That's probably why people often like mother like son, or the apple doesn't fall far from the tree 

## What kind of international news inspires people?

I'm not someone who follows international news very closely, but from what I have seen. People are often inspired by stories about kindness, especially during tough times like natural disasters or pandemics. News like that shows the positive side of humanity and gives people a bit of hope. It also spreads quickly on social media because people enjoy seeing others helping one another
