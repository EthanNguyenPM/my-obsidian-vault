## Part 3

### At what age are children generally allowed to use social media in your country?

In my country, most children are usually allowed to use social media when they are around **12 or 13 years old**. This is often when they start secondary school and want to **stay connected** with their friends. However, many parents try to **set limits** and **monitor their children’s online activities** to make sure they use social media in a **safe and responsible way**

### Do you think people will use social media more or less in the future?

I believe people will use social media **even more** in the future. As technology continues to **develop rapidly**, social media will become more **convenient and interactive**. People will rely on it not only to **stay in touch**, but also to **get information**, **work online**, and **build online communities**.

However, I think some people may start to **limit their usage** because of problems like **screen addiction** and **mental stress**. So while overall use will probably increase, people will try to use social media in a more **balanced way**

### Why do you think bullying on the internet has become so widespread?

I think online bullying has become so widespread mainly because people can **hide behind a screen**. When they are **anonymous**, they feel less responsible for their actions, so they may **post hurtful comments** or **attack others** without thinking about the consequences.

Another reason is that social media allows negative messages to **spread quickly**. A rude comment or a cruel post can be **shared and seen by many people** in a very short time, which can make the problem much worse. Also, some people bully others to **get attention** or to **feel more powerful**.

Overall, the combination of **online anonymity** and **easy access to social media** has made cyberbullying much more common.

### What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media? 

Social media has both **positive and negative impacts** on our lives.

One major **advantage** is that it helps people **stay connected** with friends and family, even if they live far away. For example, we can **keep in touch**, send messages, or make video calls easily. It also allows users to **share information quickly** and **stay updated** with news and trends. Many students also use social media to **learn new things** and **exchange ideas** with others.

However, there are also some **serious drawbacks**. One big problem is that social media can be **time-consuming** and **addictive**. Many people **spend too much time scrolling**, which can **waste time** and reduce productivity. Another disadvantage is that it can cause **mental pressure**, especially when people **compare themselves with others** and feel **less confident**.

Overall, I believe social media is a **useful tool** if we use it **in a balanced way**, but it can become harmful if we **rely on it too much**.

### How has social media changed the way people communicate?

Social media has **completely changed** the way people communicate.

In the past, people mainly talked **face to face** or made phone calls. Now, thanks to social media, we can **stay in touch** with others **instantly**, no matter where they are. We can **send messages**, **share photos**, and **make video calls** in just a few seconds. This makes communication **faster and more convenient**.

Another big change is that people can now **connect with a wider audience**. For example, we can **join online communities**, **express our opinions**, and **interact with strangers** who have the same interests. This helps people **build relationships** and **exchange ideas** more easily.

However, social media has also made communication more **superficial**. Some people **prefer texting instead of talking**, which can **reduce real-life interaction** and lead to **weaker personal relationships**.

Overall, social media has made communication **more efficient**, but it has also changed the way people **connect emotionally**.
