## Have we been obsessed with the latest things nowadays?

Yeah, I think so. Nowadays, people are kind of obsessed with the newest releases, especially when it comes to tech products. New items usually come with upgraded features and a more modern design, but more importantly, they can boost someone’s social image.

For example, whenever a new iPhone is launched, a lot of people rush to buy it, even if their old phone still works perfectly fine. I think it’s partly because owning the latest model makes them feel more confident and socially recognized.
