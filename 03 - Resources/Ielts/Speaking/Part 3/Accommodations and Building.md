## Do people prefer living in apartments or houses in your country? Why?

I think people in my country generally prefer to live in apartments. This is because apartment buildings have better security. There are many CCTV cameras in every hallway of the apartment building. You can find guards on duty as well. Furthermore, apartment buildings are often equipped with amenities like pools, gyms, and convenience stores. Having these things in the complex makes my life more convenient.

I'd say houses are actually more popular among Vietnamese families. The main reason is that houses offer much more living space, which is essential when you have kids or elderly parents staying with you. Not only do they provide you with separate rooms for everyone, but they also come with a small yard where children can play safely. 

## Some people prefer living in a small house to living in a big house. Why is that?

The specific reasons would be different from person to person, but I think people prefer smaller homes because they are far easier to maintain. They require much less time and effort to clean, and repairs are usually relatively minor. Also, smaller homes are less expensive. This makes them a more suitable choice for those on a budget.

**I actually think bigger houses are far better for families with children.** **What makes them attractive is that each family member can have their own bedroom** without having to share, which promotes better privacy and reduces conflicts. Moreover, **if you have elderly parents staying with you, a spacious house allows them to have independence while remaining close to the family.** On top of that, kids can **enjoy dedicated play areas** without disturbing others, and parents get **breathing space** to relax after work
