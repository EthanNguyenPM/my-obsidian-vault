## Part 1

### Who is your favorite celebrity in your country?

I'm going to talk about Khanh Vy, the celebrity I really admire, who inspires me with her language ability. I'm not sure if you're aware, but she's a young, versatile Vietnamese girl. Although she's still in her 20s, she's already a talented MC, a well-known online personality, and, most impressively, a polyglot. I first heard about her around 9 years ago when she was still a high school student. At the time, a friend of hers recorded her speaking seven different languages in just a few minutes and posted it on Facebook. The video went viral almost immediately. I came across it because one of my friends shared it with me. Like many people, I was completely blown away by her language ability. So I started following her, and I've been a fan of hers ever since.

### Do you like any foreign celebrities?

Yes, I do. I’m a big fan of Phil Foden, who plays for Manchester City in the Premier League. I really **look up** to him because he’s a young, talented player who always works hard for his team and stays quite humble. His close control and creativity on the ball always blow me away, especially in important matches

### Would you like to be a celebrity?

I don’t want to be a celebrity because they are always in the public eye and have no private life.  
I’d rather live a normal life, focus on my studies and hobbies, and stay away from too much attention.

### Do you follow celebrity news?

No, I don’t really follow celebrity news.
I only see it sometimes on social media when a big story goes viral, but I don’t actively look for it.
I prefer to stay up to date with sports and international news instead of celebrity gossip

### What kind of famous people do you often see in the news?

Mostly singers, actors, and social media stars.
They pop up in the news for new songs, dramas, or viral videos that go viral on TikTok.
Athletes and politicians also get a lot of coverage when they win big or make big announcements.
## Part 3
### What types of people become famous in your country?


I’d say that in my country, people usually become famous by working in the entertainment industry.  
Singers, actors and TV hosts often **rise** to fame when their shows or songs go viral, and many social media influencers also turn into household names thanks to TikTok and YouTube.  
Besides that, talented athletes and successful businesspeople are increasingly seen as public figures, especially when they win major awards or build well-known brands.
