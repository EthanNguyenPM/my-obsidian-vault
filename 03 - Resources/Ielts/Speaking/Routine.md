## What's your daily routine like?

Well, I'm definitely more of a **night owl** than an early bird – my day really **comes alive after dark**. I tend to **burn the midnight oil**, **pulling all-nighters** during intensive IELTS prep phases, when my mind feels **sharpest** and most **creative**. Mornings are for **catching up on sleep** to **recharge**, then I **ease into** the afternoon with lighter tasks like triathlon planning or Obsidian note-taking. Evenings kick off with a **power nap**, followed by focused study until the **wee hours**, helping me **maximize productivity** when distractions are minimal. This routine suits my **natural body clock** perfectly



## What's your favorite time of the day?

My favorite time of day has to be the **wee hours** – that magical window between 1 and 4 AM when everything feels **eerily quiet** and my mind is at its **sharpest**. As a **night owl**, I **thrive in the stillness**, **diving deep** into IELTS practice or triathlon planning without any distractions. It's when I can **truly focus** and **produce my best work**, whether **burning the midnight oil** on complex vocabulary or **unwinding** with music. Mornings just can't compete with that **golden productivity window**

## What do you usually do at this time of the day in the evening?

Around this time in the evening – say 8 to 10 PM – I'm usually in **full unwind mode**, **easing off the gas** after intensive IELTS sessions. I either **hit the gym** for light triathlon training to **get my heart rate up**, or **settle in** with Obsidian for **structured note-taking** and vocabulary review. Sometimes I **treat myself** to music discovery or Christopher Nolan films – it's my way of **recharging batteries** while **keeping momentum** for late-night study. This evening ritual helps me **stay sharp** without **burning out**

## When and how did you establish this routine?

I established this routine **around two years ago**, right when I **ramped up** my IELTS prep and discovered I'm a true **night owl** at heart. It all **kicked off** during a particularly grueling exam period – I found my focus **peaked** in the **wee hours**, so I **gradually phased in** late-night study slots while **cutting back** on early mornings. Triathlon training **fitted seamlessly** into evenings, thanks to apps like Strava for tracking. Over time, it **snowballed into** a **well-oiled routine** that **boosted my productivity** tenfold – no more fighting against my **natural rhythm**!

## Did you face any difficulties before getting yourself into this routine?


Absolutely, getting into this routine was **far from smooth sailing** – I faced **major hurdles** adapting from a chaotic schedule to disciplined night owl hours. Initially, **sleep deprivation** hit me hard after **pulling all-nighters** without proper wind-down rituals, leaving me **running on fumes** during the day. Socially, it was tricky too – friends would **catch up over coffee** in mornings while I was **catching up on sleep**, creating **social jetlag**. But through **trial and error**, I **fine-tuned** my approach: **power naps**, **blue light filters**, and **strict cut-off times** helped me **iron out the kinks** and now it's **second nature**.
