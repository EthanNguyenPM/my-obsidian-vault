## Have you ever written a story before?

### Using the PEEL method

Yes, I have written a few stories before to translate my dream into words. I wrote it as a way to practice my creativity and improve my English skills. For example, in high school, I wrote a short story about a boy who travels through different dimensions to find the solution to save the world from aliens from Mars. So overall, writing stories has been a valuable and enjoyable activity for me.

### Using the PREP

Yes, I have written one story before. The reason for this is that I enjoy expressing my ideas through creative writing because it allows me to think freely and develop my English skills. For instance, I once wrote a story about a lonely robot who wanted to understand human emotions, and it turned out to be one of my favorite pieces. So yes, story writing is something I've done, and I find it quite meaningful.

### Using PAS 

Many people struggle to express themselves creatively, especially when writing a story in a second language. It can feel intimidating because you need ideas, structures, and good vocabulary. That's why I started writing my own stories. I've written many short pieces, and they've helped me gain confidence in expressing myself more clearly.

## **Do you prefer listening to stories or reading them?**

### PEEL

To be honest, I prefer reading stories to listening to stories. It allows me to lose myself in a book and absorb information more deeply. When I read, I can slow it down, reflect, and dwell on the character's emotions. In a nutshell, reading opens up a whole new world to explore.
