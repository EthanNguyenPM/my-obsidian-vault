## Do you have an experience when you did something well? 

Honestly, there was this time **when our server went down, and I was the one who fixed it pretty quickly**. **Everyone kept asking me what had happened**, so I explained the problem and the solution. **It felt awesome to help out**, especially when people relied on me.

To be honest, **I've worked on a mobile app with my friends recently**, and **what amazed me was how smoothly everything ran**. **We divided the tasks, and ended up finishing before the deadline**. People still say it’s one of our best projects.
