## Part 3

### How are relationships today different from the past

Relationships today are quite different from those in the past, mainly because of **modern technology** and **changing lifestyles**.

In the past, people usually **met in person** through family, school, or work, and relationships often developed **more slowly**. Today, many people use **social media and dating apps** to **meet new people**, which makes it **faster and easier** to start a relationship.

Also, people nowadays tend to be more **independent** and **open-minded**. They are more willing to **express their feelings** and **make their own choices**, while in the past, relationships were more influenced by **family expectations** and **tradition**.

Overall, modern relationships are more **flexible**, but sometimes they can be less **stable** than before.

### What are the advantages/disadvantages of using dating apps?

Dating apps have both **benefits and drawbacks**.

One main **advantage** is that they make it easier to **meet new people**, especially for those who are **busy** or **shy in real life**. Users can **connect with others** who have **similar interests**, which saves time and increases the chance of **finding a good match**.

However, there are also some **disadvantages**. Relationships that start online can be more **superficial**, because people often judge others by their **profile photos** rather than their real personality. In addition, dating apps can lead to **dishonest profiles** and **emotional disappointment** if people are not honest about who they are.

Overall, dating apps are **convenient**, but they should be used **carefully and responsibly**.

### Do you think online dating helps people find true love?

I think online dating can help some people find **true love**, but it doesn’t work for everyone.

On the positive side, dating apps allow people to **meet a wider range of people** and **connect with others** who have **similar interests and values**. This increases the chance of **finding a suitable partner**, especially for people who don’t have many opportunities to **meet new people** in real life.

However, online dating can also be **unreliable**, because some users **don’t show their real personality** or **use fake profiles**. As a result, relationships that start online may be more **uncertain** and sometimes lead to **disappointment**.

Overall, I believe online dating can be a **useful tool**, but **true love** still depends on **real communication and trust**.

### Is it common for people to meet partners through apps like Tinder?

Yes, it is becoming quite **common**, especially among young adults. Many people use apps like Tinder to **meet new people** and **start relationships** because it is a **quick and convenient way** to connect with others. With busy lifestyles, online dating has become a **popular option** for finding a partner.

### What do you think about one-night stands?

**Well, to be completely honest**, I think one-night stands are **quite a common phenomenon** in today's fast-paced society.

With the rise of dating apps and changing social norms, many young people nowadays tend to **prioritise physical attraction** and **instant gratification** over long-term emotional commitment.

From my perspective, as long as both parties are **consenting adults**, fully aware of the situation and **respect each other's boundaries**, then I don't see it as inherently wrong.

However, it can sometimes lead to **emotional complications** or **unrealistic expectations**, especially if one person secretly hopes for something more serious

### Who should pay on the first date, the man or the woman?

Well, I'd say this is quite a controversial topic, and **it really depends on various factors**. In my opinion, there's no one-size-fits-all answer, but I can share some common perspectives.

In the past, it was **deeply rooted in tradition** that the man would **foot the bill** / **cover the entire cost** as a **gesture of courtesy** and to **show good manners**. However, nowadays, especially in **progressive societies** and among **younger generations**, **going Dutch** or **splitting the bill** has become **increasingly common** and is widely **seen as a fair approach**.

Personally, I believe the best way is to **discuss it openly** beforehand or simply **take turns paying** depending on the situation. What really matters is **mutual respect** and whether both people feel **comfortable with the arrangement**.
