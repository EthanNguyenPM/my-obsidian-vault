I’d like to talk about a skill I learned from my father, which is how to stay calm and make rational decisions under pressure.

Growing up, I used to be quite impulsive. Whenever something went wrong, I would react emotionally without thinking things through. However, my father is the complete opposite. He’s always composed and rarely lets his emotions take control.

One day, when I was facing a difficult situation at school, I was extremely frustrated and didn’t know what to do. Instead of giving me direct advice, he asked me to sit down and break the problem into smaller parts. He told me that most problems seem overwhelming only because we look at them as a whole.

Over time, I gradually developed the habit of analyzing situations more logically instead of reacting immediately. Although it may not sound like a technical skill, I believe emotional control and rational thinking are crucial life skills.

Even now, whenever I encounter challenges, I try to pause, reflect, and think things through — just like he taught me.
