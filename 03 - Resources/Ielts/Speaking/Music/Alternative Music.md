I’m really into Alternative rock because it feels more authentic and emotionally expressive compared to mainstream pop music.  
What I like most about this genre is its combination of powerful guitar riffs, meaningful lyrics, and raw emotions.  
Whenever I listen to alternative rock, I feel energized and emotionally connected to the music.  
I also enjoy the fact that many alternative rock songs talk about personal struggles, identity, and social issues, which makes the music feel more relatable and thought-provoking

- raw emotions = cảm xúc thô, thật
- emotionally expressive = giàu biểu cảm
- powerful guitar riffs = đoạn guitar mạnh
- meaningful lyrics = lời bài hát ý nghĩa
- relatable = dễ đồng cảm
- thought-provoking = khiến suy ngẫm
- commercialized music = nhạc quá thị trường
- intense atmosphere = không khí mạnh, dữ dội
- energizing = tiếp thêm năng lượng
