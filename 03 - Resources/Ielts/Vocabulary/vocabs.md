The video frequently warns against simply memorizing lists of synonyms without understanding their **specific meanings and appropriate contexts** (3:01, 6:48, 9:04, 11:02, 12:20, 20:25). Misusing synonyms can lead to lower scores, even if the word itself is considered "advanced." The speaker advises checking dictionaries for precise meanings and practicing using words in context (12:30).
