# 50 IELTS Synonyms You Need to Know
> Source: IELTS Advantage — *50 Synonyms You NEED To Know to Pass The IELTS Test*
> 📥 [Free PDF with extra synonyms](https://bit.ly/4914Lnc)

***

## ⚠️ Golden Rule Before You Start
> **Never blindly swap synonyms.** Always consider:
> - **Meaning**: Does it match the context exactly?
> - **Grammar**: Does it fit the sentence structure?
> - **Register**: Is it formal enough for Task 2?
>
> A simpler word used *correctly* scores higher than an advanced word used *wrongly*.

## 👥 People & Society

| # | Word | Synonyms | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | **People** | individuals, the public | *The public* = collective group |
| 7 | **Society** | the community, the general populace | *Populace* = very formal, academic |
| 10 | **Children** | youngsters, the youth, young people, minors | ⚠️ *Teenagers* = 13–17 only; *toddlers* = 2–3 yrs old |
| 11 | **Students** | learners, pupils, scholars | *Pupils* = school-age only; *scholars* = high-achieving/university |
| 17 | **Companies** | corporations, businesses | *Corporations* = large; *businesses* = any size |
| 18 | **Individual** *(n.)* | person, citizen | *Citizen* emphasises role in society |
| 29 | **Teachers** | educators, tutors, instructors | *Tutors* = private/small group; *instructors* = practical skills |

| 39 | **Community** | society, populace | *Populace* = very formal |

## 💡 Key Adjectives

| # | Word | Synonyms | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 2 | **Important** | crucial, vital | *Crucial* = decisive; *vital* = necessary for life/success |
| 3 | **Good** | beneficial, positive | *Beneficial* = provides advantage; *positive* = direct academic alternative |
| 13 | **Effective** | productive, potent | *Potent* = strong and powerful effect |
| 14 | **Necessary** | essential, imperative | *Imperative* = very strong, urgent, formal |
| 15 | **Different** | various, diverse | *Diverse* = wide range of distinct types |
| 20 | **Modern** | contemporary, present-day | *Contemporary* = more formal/academic |
| 31 | **Social** | communal, societal, public | *Societal* = very formal, relates to society as a whole |
| 40 | **Economic** | financial, monetary | *Monetary* = specifically about a country's money/policy |
| 41 | **Personal** | individual, private | *Private* = not for public consumption |
| 43 | **Significant** | substantial, considerable | Both suggest large amount; good for Task 1 data |
| 44 | **Possible** | feasible, viable | *Feasible* = practical; *viable* = capable of working successfully |
| 45 | **Available** | accessible, obtainable | *Accessible* = easy to use; *obtainable* = able to be got |
| 47 | **Popular** | widespread, prevalent | ⚠️ Don't confuse *popular* (liked) with *common* (frequent) |
| 48 | **Common** | widespread, ubiquitous, frequent | ⚠️ Spell *ubiquitous* correctly or use *frequent* instead |
| 49 | **General** | overall, broadly, widespread | Good for introductions and conclusions |

## 🔧 Key Verbs

| # | Word | Synonyms | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 4 | **Develop** | cultivate, foster | *Cultivate* = gradual/careful; *foster* = encourage to grow |
| 5 | **Increase** | boost, raise | Even simple synonyms show range — don't fear them |
| 12 | **Improve** | enhance, ameliorate | ⚠️ *Ameliorate* is often misspelled → lower score risk |
| 22 | **Help** | assist, aid | *Aid* often used with *in* or as a noun |
| 23 | **Provide** | supply, offer | *Supply* = fulfilling a need; *offer* = making something available |
| 24 | **Believe** | argue, contend | *Contend* = very formal for putting forward an argument |
| 25 | **Create** | generate, establish | *Generate* = jobs/revenue; *establish* = long-term setup |
